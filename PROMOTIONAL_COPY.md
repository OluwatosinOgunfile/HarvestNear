# HarvestNearU Launch Copy

## Campaign Platform

**Campaign theme:** Fresh Local Produce, Found Here.
**Consumer message:** Search naturally with Amara, browse every nearby farm across eighteen produce categories, and follow every order from a verified farm through to receipt.
**Farmer message:** Sell from the web or your phone and get paid quickly. Most payouts are released automatically about an hour after the buyer confirms receipt.
**Primary CTA:** Shop nearby harvests.
**Farmer CTA:** Start selling on HarvestNearU.

## Consumer Launch Caption

The freshest food may be closer than you think. Ask Amara to help you find matching harvests, browse every verified farm near you, and shop across eighteen categories, from leafy greens and peppers to honey, dairy, fish and palm oil. Something sold out? Ask to be told the moment it is back. Choose delivery or pickup, pay securely, and follow each item through to receipt. Shop local on the web or the HarvestNearU mobile app.

## Farmer Recruitment Caption

Your next customer could be nearby, and your money should not be far behind. Create one account for multiple farms, publish and edit listings, update stock from the field, and manage fulfilment from your phone. Most payouts are automatic: at or below NGN 50,000 they are released about an hour after the buyer confirms receipt, with larger amounts approved by an administrator. A flat 10% platform and processing fee is shown to you on the pricing form before you publish, so your net is never a surprise.

## WhatsApp Broadcast

Fresh harvests are closer. Search with Amara, save your favourite produce, and browse every verified farm near you across eighteen categories. Ask to be notified when a sold-out item returns. Choose doorstep delivery, farm pickup, or arrange delivery with the farmer, then follow every update to receipt. Visit **harvestnearu.com**.

## Product Proof Points

- Eighteen produce categories spanning field crops, livestock, fish, dairy, honey, oils, and planting material
- A browsable directory of every farm, showing sold-out produce alongside what is available
- One-tap restock alerts on a sold-out listing, sent when the farmer puts stock back
- Verified farm storefronts with address, ratings, feedback, and current produce
- Live remaining-stock controls and automatic out-of-stock status
- Proximity-first discovery expressed as estimated walking time
- Free farm maps and routed directions from a current or saved location
- Paystack checkout with optional administrator-reviewed bank transfer
- Distance-priced doorstep delivery, free farm pickup, or delivery arranged with the farmer
- Separate tracking and receipt confirmation for each product in an order
- Printable order receipts and farmer payout statements
- Automatic farmer payouts at or below NGN 50,000, released after a short confirmation window, with larger amounts approved by an administrator
- A flat 10% platform and processing fee, disclosed on the pricing form before a listing is published
- Payout accounts confirmed against the bank before they can be saved
- Farmer identity checked by a person against the payout account, with identity numbers stored only as a hash and last four digits
- Versioned terms and privacy acceptance, self-service data export, and enforced retention periods
- Persistent basket, favourites, account credit, real-time notifications, and email preferences
- Email or Google sign-in with guided profile setup
- Amara-assisted marketplace search grounded in available produce
- Support-ticket conversations and transparent refund status
- Native mobile access for customers and farmers, with secure web-console access for staff
- Mobile listing editing, stock updates, farm profiles, fulfilment history, reviews, payouts, and statements
- Android home-screen shortcuts that jump straight to the basket, orders, workspace, or a new listing

1. **Teaser:** Something fresh is growing near you.
2. **Reveal:** Introduce HarvestNearU and its consumer benefit.
3. **Supply:** Recruit and introduce verified local farmers.
4. **Proof:** Share storefronts, live availability, verified ratings, and fulfilled items.
5. **Habit:** Publish weekly harvest drops and seasonal reminders.
